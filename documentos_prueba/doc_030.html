Replication Data for 'Explicit Geometry: Integrating Music and Mathematics in Architectural Design'

Autores: Alva, Aleix

Palabras clave: Arts and Humanities | Mathematical Sciences | Other | Geometry | Architecture | Proportion | Harmony | Music

Fecha: 2024-06-03

Identificador: doi:10.34810/DATA1063

This dataset provides supplementary information to the article "Explicit Geometry: Integrating Music and Mathematics in Architectural Design". It includes Asymptote-graphics code to generate the geometric constructions and also a set of 3D WebGL files to interactively explore them. It includes SoX code to generate audio files with the exact frequencies of the model. The audio files are also included in WAV
