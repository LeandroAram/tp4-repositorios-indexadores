Translations and Reviews in Iberoamerican Modernist Periodicals

Identificador: doi:10.34810/DATA1108

This dataset records translations and reviews of foreign works published in Spanish or Latin American modernist journals (understanding 'modernist' in the anglophone sense). Thus, it currently includes all translations or reviews from the following journals: La Revista (Barcelona) from 1915 to 1936; the second period of Proa (Buenos Aires) from 1924 to 1926, and Sur (Buenos Aires) from 1931 to 1939. The dataset will be expanded to include more Iberoamerican journals in the future.
